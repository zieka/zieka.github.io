Atman Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the Atman apps for macOS and iOS. Atman has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and we do not run our own servers. Data the app keeps off your device is stored with Apple's iCloud.

On your device

Your list of custom celebrations and your settings are stored on your device.

Your private iCloud

When you are signed in to iCloud, Atman stores your daily celebration pick (including the name of a custom celebration, if you pick one), the friend groups you have joined, and the nicknames you give them in your private iCloud database, so your devices stay in sync. On macOS, your streaks also sync through iCloud. We cannot access your private iCloud data.

Friend groups

Friend groups are optional. When you create a group, its code is stored in Atman's public iCloud database so others can join with it, along with the Apple-assigned identifier described below.

When you pick a celebration while in a group, Atman stores a vote in that public database. A vote contains:

  • the name of the celebration you picked (custom celebrations are stored only as "A personal celebration");
  • the day;
  • a one-way hash of the group code;
  • an identifier Apple assigns to your iCloud account for Atman. It is not your Apple ID, name, or email.

Group members see only how many people picked each celebration, never who picked what. We can view these records through Apple's developer tools. We use them only to show group results, and we do not sell, share, or combine them with other data.

Links to other services

"Learn more" opens a Google search for the celebration in your browser. Google's privacy policy applies to that search. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Changing your pick replaces your vote for that day, and leaving a group deletes today's vote. Votes from earlier days stay in Atman's public iCloud database; they contain no name, email, or other contact details. You can delete Atman's private iCloud data in your device's iCloud storage settings.

Children

Atman is not directed to children under 13.

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
