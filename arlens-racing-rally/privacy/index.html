Arlen's Racing Rally Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers the Arlen's Racing Rally app for iPhone and iPad. The app does not collect any data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, it does not connect to the internet, and we do not run any servers.

On your device

Your game progress (finished levels, medals, unlocked parts and your garage choices) and your settings (sound, music and haptics) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Crash reports

If you turn on Share With App Developers in your device's Analytics & Improvements settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They help us fix bugs and do not identify you. Apple provides them under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through zieka.vercel.app.
